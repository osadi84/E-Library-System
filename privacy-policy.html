Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

My e-Library is a learning and portfolio project. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to the information you type into it.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter (account details, cart, wishlist, orders, reviews and contact messages) is stored only in your own browser using localStorage. There is no server, no database in the cloud and no analytics or tracking. Nothing is sent to the project author or any third party.

What this means for you
  • Your data is visible to anyone who can use your browser profile on your device.
  • Passwords are hashed in the browser, but client-side storage is not a secure vault. Please do not use a real password or real personal details.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using Admin → Database tools → Reset demo data, deletes everything.
