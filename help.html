Frequently Asked Questions

To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button at the top right of the page or visit our registration page. You'll need to provide your name, email address, and create a password.

If you've forgotten your password, go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password." You'll receive an email with instructions to reset your password.

Our e-Library is accessible on all devices including computers, tablets, and smartphones. We also have dedicated apps for iOS and Android devices available in their respective app stores.

To borrow an e-book, simply find the book you want in our catalog, click on it, and then click the "Borrow" button. The book will be added to your personal library for the loan period (typically 14 days).

Yes, most books in our collection can be downloaded for offline reading. Look for the download icon (↓) next to the book in your library. Note that some publisher restrictions may apply to certain titles.

Standard members can borrow up to 5 books at a time. Premium members have a limit of 10 books. You can return books early to borrow additional titles.

We support EPUB, PDF, and MOBI formats. Our reading app automatically selects the best format for your device. You can also choose your preferred format in the settings.

You can renew books from your "My Library" section. Click on the book and select "Renew" if available. Note that renewals are subject to availability - if another user has placed a hold on the book, you may need to return it.

Basic membership is free and gives you access to thousands of titles. We also offer a premium membership with additional benefits for a small monthly fee. Some special collections or new releases may require separate purchase.

We welcome book recommendations! You can suggest titles by visiting the "Contact Us" page and selecting "Book Suggestion" from the dropdown menu. Our acquisitions team reviews all suggestions regularly.
